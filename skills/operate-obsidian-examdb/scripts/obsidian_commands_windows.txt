Replace C:\Projects\Exam_Database with the actual repository path.

Initialize ExamDB directories/schema (only for a new empty database):
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Projects\Exam_Database\scripts\windows\examdb.ps1" init

Start practice UI:
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Projects\Exam_Database\scripts\windows\start-practice-server.ps1"

Collect QSTheory materials:
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Projects\Exam_Database\scripts\windows\collect-recent.ps1" -Source qstheory

Collect People Commentary:
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Projects\Exam_Database\scripts\windows\collect-recent.ps1" -Source people-commentary

Generate weekly report:
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Projects\Exam_Database\scripts\windows\generate-weekly-report.ps1"

Preview DeepSeek retagging:
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Projects\Exam_Database\scripts\windows\retag-articles.ps1" -Source qstheory -Limit 10

Preview Markdown metadata synchronization:
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Projects\Exam_Database\scripts\windows\sync-articles.ps1" -Source qstheory -OnlyChanged

Fenbi manual login:
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Projects\Exam_Database\scripts\windows\fenbi-login.ps1"

Discover Fenbi papers:
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Projects\Exam_Database\scripts\windows\discover-fenbi-papers.ps1" -LabelId 1 -PaperKind xingce

Fetch and import one Fenbi paper:
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Projects\Exam_Database\scripts\windows\fetch-fenbi-paper.ps1" -PaperId 222388 -Import
